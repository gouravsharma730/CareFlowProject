# Referral Management Policy

**MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS**
**OPERATIONS POLICY MANUAL**
**SECTION 3: REFERRAL MANAGEMENT & TRIAGE**
**Effective Date:** 14 January 2025
**Document ID:** MHP-POL-OP-034
**Owner:** Dr. Aris Thorne, Chief Medical Officer

---

### 3.1 Purpose and Scope
3.1.1 This policy governs the processing, triage, tracking, and escalation of all outpatient referrals across all 14 Meridian Health Partners (MHP) clinic sites. 
3.1.2 Compliance is mandatory for all clinical, administrative, and management personnel. 
3.1.3 The Meridian Referral Tracking System (MRTS) serves as the sole system of record for all incoming, internal, and outgoing referral lifecycles.

### 3.2 Referral Pathways and Primary-Care Sign-Off
3.2.1 All patients entering the MHP network for specialty care must possess a documented clinical origin within MRTS.
3.2.2 Direct Self-Referrals without prior primary-care provider (PCP) sign-off are strictly limited to the following four outpatient specialties: Dermatology, Physical Therapy, Optometry, and General Gynecology.
3.2.3 All other MHP specialties—including Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedic Surgery, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Oncology, Psychiatry, and Urogynecology—require formal PCP sign-off, inclusive of a completed clinical summary and relevant diagnostic attachments, prior to initial booking.
3.2.4 Any direct referral received by MRTS for a specialty requiring PCP sign-off without the requisite documentation shall be automatically routed to the originating clinic's designated Care Coordinator for PCP remediation within 24 hours.

### 3.3 Urgency Bands and Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
3.3.1 MRTS enforces strict turnaround SLAs based on four distinct clinical urgency bands assigned at the point of entry.
3.3.2 Routine Referrals: Must be actioned, triaged, and communicated to the patient for booking within exactly five (5) business days from submission.
3.3.3 Priority Referrals: Must be actioned, triaged, and communicated to the patient for booking within exactly two (2) business days from submission.
3.3.4 Urgent Referrals: Must be actioned, triaged, and communicated to the patient for booking within exactly one (1) business day, defined explicitly as by the end of the next working day (17:00 local time) from submission.
3.3.5 Emergent Presentations: Emergent clinical presentations are never processed through the MHP referral management pathway. Any referral identified as emergent by intake staff or MRTS keyword filters shall be instantly diverted; staff must direct the patient to emergency care immediately via emergency dispatch or direct emergency department transfer protocols.

### 3.4 SLA Overrides and Governance
3.4.1 Standard MRTS SLA timeframes are immutable except under explicit override protocols authorized by designated executive roles.
3.4.2 The following roles possess sole authority to issue an SLA override:
(a) Dr. Aris Thorne, Chief Medical Officer;
(b) Elena Vance, Vice President of Clinical Operations;
(c) Regional Medical Directors (specifically: Dr. Marcus Vance, Northern Region; Dr. Sarah Lin, Southern Region; Dr. Kevin Patel, Central Region).
3.4.3 All SLA overrides must be logged within MRTS with a mandatory clinical justification text block exceeding 50 characters, referencing the overriding officer's employee identification number.

### 3.5 Escalation Ladder
3.5.1 Failure to meet MRTS urgency band SLAs triggers an automated escalation sequence through a defined hierarchy of named role titles.
3.5.2 Level 1 Escalation (Breach + 4 Hours): Notification is sent via SMS and high-priority internal dashboard alert to the site-specific Clinic Operations Manager (COM), requiring manual review within two business hours.
3.5.3 Level 2 Escalation (Breach + 24 Hours): Notification is escalated to the Regional Practice Director (RPD), who must reassign the un-actioned referral to an alternate available specialist within the MHP network within four business hours.
3.5.4 Level 3 Escalation (Breach + 48 Hours): Notification is escalated to the Vice President of Clinical Operations (VPCO), who initiates a formal departmental audit and direct intervention.
3.5.5 Level 4 Escalation (Breach + 72 Hours): Final critical escalation to the Chief Medical Officer (CMO) and Chief Executive Officer (CEO), triggering a mandatory root-cause analysis report submitted to the MHP Quality and Safety Committee within five business days.

### 3.6 Documented Exceptions to the Standard Pathway
3.6.1 Exception One: Inter-Facility Continuity of Care. Referrals generated as a direct consequence of an inpatient discharge from an MHP-affiliated partner hospital bed for an existing surgical or medical follow-up do not require PCP sign-off, regardless of specialty. Such referrals bypass standard intake queues and are ingested directly into the Priority urgency band, subject to a mandatory 24-hour booking SLA enforced by MRTS.
3.6.2 Exception Two: Active Clinical Trials and Research Protocols. Patients enrolling in institutional review board (IRB)-approved clinical trials managed within MHP research units are exempt from standard specialty routing rules. Their referral pathways are governed exclusively by the Principal Investigator's protocol-specific intake schema, which supersedes MRTS default SLA timings and PCP sign-off requirements, provided a valid protocol identifier is tagged in MRTS at creation.
3.6.3 Exception Three: Standing Pediatric Behavioral Health Agreements. Pediatric patients (ages 0–17) referred from designated county-contracted school district health nurses under the MHP Youth Access Initiative are permitted direct intake into Pediatric Psychiatry and Behavioral Health without prior PCP sign-off. These referrals are categorized as Priority by default and must be actioned within exactly two (2) business days, with administrative intake documentation handled post-booking by the MHP Community Liaison team.

### 3.7 Audit and Compliance
3.7.1 MRTS maintains an immutable audit trail of every referral transaction, view, edit, override, and escalation event.
3.7.2 Monthly compliance reports detailing SLA adherence rates by clinic site, specialty, and urgency band shall be generated automatically by MRTS on the first calendar day of each month and delivered directly to the Office of the Chief Medical Officer.
3.7.3 Failure of any clinic site to maintain an aggregate monthly SLA compliance rate of at least 98.5% across all urgency bands shall result in a mandatory operational review conducted by the Regional Practice Director and the Compliance Directorate within 14 calendar days of report generation.
