# Pre-Authorisation Requirement Matrix

# MERIDIAN HEALTH PARTNERS
## OPERATIONAL POLICY MANUAL
### SECTION 4: CLAIMS, BILLING, AND INSURANCE
#### SUB-SECTION 4.8: PRE-AUTHORISATION AND PLAN TIER REQUIREMENTS

---

### 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This policy establishes the mandatory pre-authorisation requirements, approval windows, required clinical documentation, and appeal routes for all outpatient procedures, imaging, laboratory tests, and specialist consultations performed across all fourteen Meridian Health Partners sites.

1.2 The rules set forth in this document apply strictly to four insurance plans: MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE, and CIVIC-BASE.

1.3 Adherence to these pre-authorisation schedules is mandatory for all clinic administrative staff, billing specialists, and referring physicians. Failure to secure timely pre-authorisation shall result in uncollectible claim balances for which the patient shall not be held liable pursuant to Meridian Health Partners financial indemnity guidelines.

---

### 2.0 GENERAL RULES BY SERVICE TYPE

2.1 **Consultations:** All specialist consultations (CARD-CONS, ORTH-CONS, DERM-CONS, ENDO-CONS, GAST-CONS, PULM-CONS, NEUR-CONS, ENT-CONS) never require pre-authorisation under any of the four designated plans.

2.2 **Lab Work:** All laboratory tests and diagnostic panels (DERM-BIOP, ENDO-PANL, PULM-PFT, ENT-AUDI) never require pre-authorisation under any of the four designated plans.

2.3 **Imaging:** Diagnostic imaging services require pre-authorisation depending on the specific insurance plan tier, in accordance with the approval windows defined in Section 3.0.

2.4 **Procedures:** Surgical, invasive, and diagnostic procedures require pre-authorisation under every plan, subject to the plan-specific approval windows defined in Section 3.0. Certain procedures are excluded entirely from specific plan coverage as detailed herein.

---

### 3.0 PROCEDURE-SPECIFIC SCHEDULES

#### 3.1 Cardiology (CARD)

3.1.1 **CARD-CONS (Cardiology Consultation, list price $220):** Pre-authorisation is not required under MERIDIAN-GOLD, MERIDIAN-SILVER, MERIDIAN-BRONZE, or CIVIC-BASE. Required clinical documentation: none. Appeal route if denied: Level 1 Medical Director Peer Review via the Meridian Utilization Management Office, followed by External Independent Medical Review administered by the State Health Insurance Commissioner.

3.1.2 **CARD-ECHO (Echocardiogram, list price $950):** Pre-authorisation requirements by plan: MERIDIAN-GOLD: not required; MERIDIAN-SILVER: required (approval window: 3 business days); MERIDIAN-BRONZE: required (approval window: 5 business days); CIVIC-BASE: required (approval window: 5 business days). Required clinical documentation: physician referral notes, documented cardiac murmur or symptoms of heart failure, and prior electrocardiogram results. Appeal route if denied: Level 1 Medical Director Peer Review, followed by External Independent Medical Review.

3.1.3 **CARD-CATH (Cardiac Catheterisation, list price $7,800):** Pre-authorisation requirements by plan: MERIDIAN-GOLD: required (approval window: 2 business days); MERIDIAN-SILVER: required (approval window: 3 business days); MERIDIAN-BRONZE: required (approval window: 5 business days); CIVIC-BASE: not covered at all (pre-authorisation unavailable; claim will be rejected at intake). Required clinical documentation: positive stress test results, cardiologist consultation notes, and recent coronary angiogram or echocardiogram imaging. Appeal route if denied: Level 1 Medical Director Peer Review, followed by External Independent Medical Review.
