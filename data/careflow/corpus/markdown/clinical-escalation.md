# Clinical Escalation and Scope-of-Practice Standard

**STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: MHP-SOP-401**
**TITLE:** Patient Communication Scope of Practice for Non-Clinical Personnel and Automated Systems
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** October 1, 2024
**ISSUED BY:** Office of Clinical Operations, Meridian Health Partners
**APPLIES TO:** All 14 Meridian Health Partners outpatient sites, central scheduling, patient access center, and automated digital interfaces.

---

### 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1** This standard defines the strict operational boundaries governing communications between patients and non-clinical personnel (including receptionists, schedulers, billing staff, and administrative assistants) as well as automated patient-facing systems (including interactive voice response, web chatbots, and automated SMS platforms) across all Meridian Health Partners (MHP) locations.

**1.2** Non-clinical staff and automated systems are strictly prohibited from providing medical advice, interpreting clinical data, or making clinical recommendations.

---

### 2. PROHIBITED COMMUNICATION CATEGORIES

Non-clinical staff and automated systems shall not provide substantive answers to, or engage in clinical discussion regarding, the following five mandatory routing categories. Every inquiry falling into these categories must be immediately transferred to a licensed clinician or queued for clinical callback in accordance with Section 4.

*   **2.1 Category A: Symptom Interpretation.** Any inquiry regarding the origin, severity, diagnosis, or potential cause of physical or psychological symptoms.
*   **2.2 Category B: Medication Questions.** Any inquiry concerning the purpose, side effects, interactions, or appropriateness of prescribed or over-the-counter medications.
*   **2.3 Category C: Dosage Instructions.** Any inquiry regarding the amount, frequency, timing, or method of administration for any medication.
*   **2.4 Category D: Test-Result Meaning.** Any inquiry regarding the clinical significance, normality, implications, or follow-up requirements of laboratory, imaging, or diagnostic test results.
*   **2.5 Category E: Conditional Inquiries ("Should I").** Any inquiry phrased using the conditional framing "should I" (e.g., "Should I stop taking this pill?", "Should I come in today?", "Should I go to the emergency room?").

---

### 3. PERMITTED COMMUNICATIONS

Non-clinical staff and automated systems are restricted solely to the following administrative functions:

*   **3.1** Scheduling, rescheduling, or canceling appointments within parameters established by the site medical director.
*   **3.2** Confirming demographic information, insurance coverage, and billing inquiries.
*   **3.3** Providing factual operational data, such as facility operating hours, physical address, parking instructions, and general provider names/specialties.
*   **3.4** Facilitating the transfer of calls or electronic messages to the appropriate clinical triage queue.

---

### 4. MANDATORY DEFLECTION PROTOCOL AND SCRIPT

**4.1** When a patient presents a query falling into Categories A through E (Section 2), the non-clinical staff member or automated system must immediately cease informational discussion and deploy the standardized deflection script without modification.

**4.2 Exact Deflection Script:**
> *"I am a non-clinical staff member [or: an automated assistant] and I am not permitted to provide medical advice, interpret health information, or answer questions about your care. Let me connect you directly with a licensed clinician or transfer your question to our clinical triage team so they can assist you properly."*

**4.3** Following the delivery of the script, phone staff must warm-transfer the call to the site’s designated clinical triage queue (Extension 4400) within 60 seconds. Automated systems must route the digital ticket to the electronic health record (EHR) clinical inbox tagged with "Priority-Clinical Routing" within 90 seconds.

---

### 5. ZERO-EXCEPTION POLICY

**5.1 Absolute Rule:** The prohibitions and deflection requirements outlined in this standard have **zero exceptions**. 

**5.2 Specific Non-Exceptions:** This rule applies without deviation under all circumstances, including but not limited to:
*   **5.2.1** Repeat callers who state they have asked the same question previously and received answers from non-clinical staff.
*   **5.2.2** Callers who explicitly state they are physicians, nurses, pharmacists, or other licensed healthcare professionals.
*   **5.2.3** Emergencies, urgent situations, or callers expressing acute distress (such callers must be immediately routed to 911 or transferred to the on-site triage nurse if on premises).
*   **5.2.4** Inquiries regarding family members, dependents, or proxy accounts.

---

### 6. COMPLIANCE AND AUDITING

**6.1** Compliance with MHP-SOP-401 shall be audited monthly by the Meridian Health Partners Quality Assurance Committee via random sampling of 50 recorded patient phone calls and 50 automated chat transcripts per site.

**6.2** Any non-clinical staff member found to have provided unauthorized clinical information shall be subject to mandatory retraining within 5 business days for a first infraction, and progressive disciplinary action up to and including termination for subsequent infractions.
